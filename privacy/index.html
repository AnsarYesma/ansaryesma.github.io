Kernel Workspace

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Purpose

Kernel Workspace is a private, owner-operated integration for authorized Google Workspace services. It is not a public consumer application.

Information accessed

With the owner’s explicit authorization, Kernel Workspace may access Gmail messages, Calendar events, Google Drive files, Google Docs, and Google Sheets data needed to perform a requested task.

Use and disclosure

Google user data is used only to provide the requested personal productivity function. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. Data is not transferred to other parties except when the owner explicitly requests an action that requires it, such as sending an email or sharing a file.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored on the owner’s private server with restricted access. The integration uses OAuth authorization and requests only the scopes needed for its Google Workspace functions. The owner can revoke access at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed to complete the owner’s requested task. The owner may request deletion of locally stored integration data and may revoke Google authorization at any time.
